# How to play Goat It?

## 🐐 The concept

**Goat It is a deduction game hosted by a Game Master (GM).**

A question is put to the players, but they may not necessarily know the answer. Their goal is to find it by discussing, comparing their hypotheses, and gradually ruling out possibilities through the GM’s answers.

> **The goal isn’t to know. The goal is to find out.**

Your knowledge can help, but it isn’t essential: what matters is reasoning together and asking the right questions.

**Only one person uses the app: the GM.**
It provides them with the question, the answer, the context, the anecdotes, and the sources needed to host the investigation.

Goat It can be played with **as few as 2 people: 1 GM and 1 player**, but investigations generally become more interesting with a larger group. The GM role can be passed on after each investigation to vary the hosting style.

---

## ❓ The golden rule

The GM answers only **Yes**, **No**, or chooses between **two options**.

Questions must therefore be answerable with a binary response.

> ✅ “Is it an animal?”
>
> ✅ “Is it a mammal?”
>
> ✅ “Is it a man or a woman?”
>
> ❌ “What breed is the animal?”

Players may ask as many questions as they like, in any order, discuss freely, and suggest their hypotheses.

> “Is it something alive?”\
> ↳ **No**
>
> “Is it an object?”\
> ↳ **Yes**
>
> “Is it made by humans?”\
> ↳ **Yes**

Every answer from the GM is useful information for the entire group. A wrong lead or guess carries no penalty: it also helps rule out possibilities.

**Card:**

- Theme: Animals
- Category: Trivia
- Difficulty: Medium

**GM:** "Here's your card: theme **Animals**, category **Trivia**, difficulty **Medium**. Question: which animal once forced the closure of a runway at Nice airport in 2000? Over to you!"

**Player 1:** "Can this animal be eaten?"

**GM:** "Yes."

**Player 2:** "What color is it?"

**GM:** "Open question! I can only answer Yes or No, or pick between two options."

**Player 2:** "Rather green or rather gray?"

**GM:** "Gray."

**Player 3:** "An elephant, then!"

**GM:** "No! But keep investigating by asking questions."

**Player 1:** "Is it an animal common in the area around Nice airport?"

*🔹 The GM takes the time to reread the context and anecdotes on the card to answer this question…*

**GM:** "Yes, quite common!"

**Player 3:** "Does it have teeth?"

**GM:** "Yes… but that's not a good lead, it won't help your thinking…"

*The group is stuck.*

**GM:** "I'll help you since you seem stuck. A hint: this animal is rather slow…"

**Player 2:** "Snails?"

**GM:** "Exactly, they're snails!"

*🔹 The GM can read the rest of the card to close the investigation and give the context of the question.*

**GM:** "Let me explain the context: in October 2000, after heavy rains, thousands of snails invaded the tarmac of Nice airport. Fun fact: three kilometers of runway had to be cleaned!"

*🔹 The GM can then move on to the next question, or hand the GM role over to another player for the next investigation.*

## 🎭 The GM guides the investigation

Before starting, the GM may announce **the category, theme, and difficulty** to give players an initial framework for their thinking.

They answer honestly and carefully, so that players can rely on their answers to move the deduction forward. But they are also free to adapt the way the game is hosted.

The GM may, in particular:

- rephrase a question that does not clearly call for a binary answer;
- correct a misunderstanding or misinterpretation;
- point out that a lead seems unlikely to be relevant;
- give a hint if the group gets stuck;
- accept wording different from that shown on the card, as long as the concept being sought is understood.

> **The GM’s role is to create a great investigation, not to trick the players.**

There is no single right way to play: each question may require a different approach.

---

## 📚 Finding the answer

When a player thinks they have found it, they can suggest their answer to the group.

The GM decides whether it is close enough to the solution. It is not necessary to use the exact wording from the card: **understanding the concept is what matters.**

Once the investigation is over, the GM may reveal the context, anecdotes, and sources associated with the answer.

> **You are not just looking for an answer. You are also discovering the story behind it.**

---

## 🚫 No external research

Players must not use Google, Wikipedia, AI, or any other external source to find the answer directly.

The investigation must take place around the table, based on discussions between players and the GM’s answers.

> **The goal is to search together, not to find the answer on your own!**

---

## 🐐 Your turn to play

Choose your GM, gather the players, and start your first investigation.

Don’t know the answer?

> **Perfect. You don’t need to know everything: you just need to ask the right questions.**
