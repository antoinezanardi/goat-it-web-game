## ✨ New

- **Game settings modal** — Fine-tune the game from a dedicated settings panel.
- **Keyboard navigation** — Play and move around the whole app without touching the mouse.
- **Spotlighted tutorial** — Every interactive tutorial step now highlights exactly where to look.

## 🛠️ Improved

- **Back home from the sidebar logo** — Clicking the game sidebar logo now takes you straight back to the home page.
- **Smarter accordions** — Opening the Context or Trivia section now scrolls it neatly into view.
- **A clean slate on re-entry** — Starting a new game now resets the questions instead of resuming the previous run.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Tutorial no longer steals your shortcuts** — Keyboard events are properly isolated while the tutorial is open.
- **The right panel, every time** — The tutorial's clue step now points to the correct accordion.
